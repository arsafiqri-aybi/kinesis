# Boundaries

## Fundamentality is contextual

“Fundamental” can mean definitionally prior, mathematically prerequisite, a physical postulate, a measurement basis, or not yet derived in an approach. Specify the meaning before assessing a concept's role.

These foundations explain the conditions for understanding motion and the limits of knowing it. This differs from covering all fundamental physical theories.

A law can be fundamental within a theory but outside this library's core because it explains **which histories occur**, rather than the elementary structures enabling motion descriptions.

## Universal claims not retained

1. **“Motion is every change.”** Too broad to distinguish spatial from nonspatial change.
2. **“Motion always changes one position.”** Insufficient for orientations and arrangements.
3. **“Every motion is a smooth curve of persistently identified objects.”** Universalizes a particular classical model.
4. **“Time ontologically precedes change.”** Does not follow merely from parametrization.
5. **“All observers share one present.”** Incompatible with special relativity.
6. **“References can erase every physical difference.”** Ignores inertial structure and geometrically defined quantities.
7. **“Continuity proves finite speed.”** Continuity ensures neither derivatives nor finite length.
8. **“Unchanging data prove rest.”** Requires identifiability conditions.
9. **“Stationary density implies zero current.”** False in general, including the quantum-circle example.
10. **“Discrete means unable to move.”** Follows only from particular joint topological and continuity assumptions.

These rejections preserve the usefulness of simple models in appropriate domains while limiting their claimed scope.

## Causality and dynamics boundaries

Before–after order does not establish causation. Motion laws do not specify initial conditions. Initial conditions and laws do not guarantee unique solutions without sufficient mathematical conditions.

Conservation is not a definition of motion. Where appropriate, symmetry–conservation relations require dynamical structure and theorem-specific conditions. Motion or geometric symmetry alone does not supply every conservation law.

## Deliberate scope limits

Core branches exclude animation techniques, motion graphics, video generation, numerical simulation, software, machine learning, motion control, biomechanics, motion-type catalogues, and Graph integration.

No new axiom system for “Motion” is constructed. Foundational differences remain visible where no valid scientific reduction is available.
